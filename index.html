COP compared with air conditioning

Model: ideal-gas air (cp 1.005 kJ/kg·K, γ 1.4). Regulators are isenthalpic with an approximate Joule–Thomson coefficient μ ≈ 0.22 − 0.0006·P K/bar. Turbines use isentropic efficiency. Vane motors take their rated shaft power and air use, scaled with supply pressure. Vortex tubes scale a reference cold ΔT with pressure ratio and cold fraction. Tank blow-down cooling, moisture and heat-exchanger sizing are not modelled. COP is cooling divided by the compressor energy that went into the air actually used (compressor power ÷ its delivery × the flow drawn); for a storage tank it uses the energy needed to fill it. Treat results as first estimates for design and CFD inputs, not as measurements.
