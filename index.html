First principles and limits

Flow balanceṁavailable = FAD × ρref × (1 − purge)
ṁmotor = N × 16.2 L/s × ρref

Same FAD density basis on both sides. Pressure alone does not create cold mass.

Work-producing expansionh₂ = h₁ − Ẇshaft / ṁ + Q̇motor / ṁ

Loaded air motors extract work. A valve is isenthalpic: h₂ = h₁. Isentropic expansion is the reversible bound.

Warm receiverdm/dt = ṁin − ṁout
d(m cᵥ T)/dt = ṁin cₚ Tᵢ − ṁout cₚ T + UA(Ta − T)
pV = mRT

Stops at the network pressure floor. Source response and thermal drift affect burst duration.

Cooling volumeM dΔ/dt = f ṁ(Δsource − Δ) − ṁambient Δ − Q̇/cₚ

Fixed ambient-density, isobaric inventory. Equal mass leaves. Capture f and ambient exchange are explicit assumptions. No spatial flow solution.

Initial charged state is an input, not a stock-system result. No compressor charging model, downstream hose or motor backpressure map. Heat leakage uses UA. Atmospheric storage has no initial pressure-driven flow.
