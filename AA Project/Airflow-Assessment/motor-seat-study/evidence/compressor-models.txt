Toughness and reliability
The perfect combination of signature Atlas Copco compressor elements and best-in-class diesel engines. Plus shock-resistant HardHat® canopy for longer lifetime and higher residual value.
Efficient transportation
Almost all product variations are below 750 kg including options, so you can tow them behind a normal passenger car without special driving license.
Variety of add-ons
With a wide range of additional equipment, you can customize your mobile compressor with air quality treatment options, extreme weather kits, built-in generator and more.
Mobile air compressors that can be towed behind a regular car
Atlas Copco small diesel air compressors are versatile and reliable partners for powering your pneumatic handheld tools, sandblasting machines and other equipment.
The shock resistant HardHat® canopy and reinforced metal frame with anti-corrosion coating not only protects inner elements, but also maintains an attractive exterior of your diesel compressor, increasing resale value of the asset.
You'll appreciate the ease of operation and transportation just as much. On top of that a wide choice of optional features is available to meet your specific needs, whatever they may be.
A truly mobile source of compressed air
Standard models weigh up to 750 kg, allowing to tow them behind a regular passenger car without special driver's license. Their portability enables easy transportation and maneuverability, reducing the need for specialized vehicles or heavy lifting equipment. Their compact design ensures they take up minimal space, making them ideal for limited workspaces.
Effortless maintenance thanks to clever design
With an optimized service design that includes, among other things, central drains and spin-on filters, you can service your Atlas Copco small mobile air compressor in less than an hour. The spillage free frame can contain 110 % of all fluids used in the process, so there is no risk to the environment.
Built-in air quality treatment options
Equipping your mobile air compressor with an aftercooler and water separator provides better air quality for efficient operation of pneumatic tools, abrasive blasting and painting equipment. You can also add a reheater if needed. Condensate drainage is automatic and requires minor attention. A bypass valve or non-return valve protects the aftercooler from freezing if the ambient temperature drops below zero.
Mobile air compressor with on-board generator for powering electric tools
An integrated generator increases the efficiency of your mobile air compressor, allowing to cover small jobsite power needs. Available in various power and voltage versions, with three outlets for quick connection. Parallel operation of the compressor and generator is allowed.
