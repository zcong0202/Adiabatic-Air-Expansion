Calculation, assumptions and sources

The design document proposed about 1°C supply air. The earlier four-motor model reproduced it with a first-law energy balance. The CFD uses that value as the nozzle inlet boundary.

The sweep holds 0.96°C fixed at every flow to compare delivery. Changing real motor flow or load can change supply temperature. These saved fields do not predict that change. A different supply temperature requires another CFD run.

T_motor = 40 − 3360 / (0.0776 × 1006)
= −3.0407°C
T_supply = T_motor + 4 = 0.9593°C

Four motors × 840 W = 3.36 kW shaft work. Four × 19.4 g/s = 77.6 g/s. Dry-air heat capacity: 1006 J/(kg·K). Reference motor inlet: 6.3 bar gauge.

Required assumptions: the loaded power/flow pair is sustained; motor heat pickup is zero; extracted shaft heat stays outside the cold stream; the cold path adds 4 K at this nominal point.

The path model uses UA = 10.0737 W/K, calibrated to that 4 K rise. This value is not a measured hardware result. It is separate from the earlier 5°C vortex-tube observation.

Calculation assumptions

Darcy–Weisbach estimates use the existing panel/model.js implementation, ideal-gas density, 40°C warm air, atmospheric cold air, 4 × 19.4 g/s and equal branches. Warm branch: 2 m / 19 mm ID. Main and branch fitting K=2; neck: 50 mm long with K=1 assumed. The neck bore and K need a drawing/test. The 1–2 kPa exhaust flag is the design-document screen, not a manufacturer limit. Warm losses exclude unmeasured internal motor inlet restrictions and regulator droop.

Cold hose-only heat leak assumes a 3 mm polymer wall (k=0.2), 19 mm insulation (k=0.035), external h=8 W/(m²·K), 30°C surroundings and −3.04°C entering air from the conditional first law. It excludes motor/body heat, joints, solar heat and startup. No moisture transport is calculated. Receiver storage uses selected source pressure → calculated running-supply floor, 40°C and isothermal inventory; this is an optimistic storage screen.

System layout, assumptions and next steps

Start with XAS 88 rental, matched air treatment and four loaded LZB46 motors. Use four wide slots as the first delivery configuration. This list covers the equipment needed to test the source assumption; it does not establish that the motors deliver the CFD’s 0.96°C air.

First acquisition

Rent one complete motor/load test station with the same air treatment. Measure power, mass flow, inlet/exhaust temperature and backpressure. Confirm a usable source point before scaling the purchase to four motors and four loads. Retain the full-system compressor quote for the later test.

Buffer choice

No cold buffer tank initially. A small cold plenum can balance flow, but adds volume, wall heat and startup delay. A warm receiver is optional for compressor response. It cannot fix inadequate average compressor flow over repeated sprays.

The CFD slider selects delivery experiments. Reducing motor inlet flow changes its operating point. To reproduce lower-flow CFD at the same temperature, qualify a stock exhaust diversion / metering arrangement and measure the delivered boundary; it is not part of the present fitted assembly.
