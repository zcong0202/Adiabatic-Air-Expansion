Attachment status: C24 3° includes the provisional 3/8-18 NPT male model and three fit coupons. The commercial Milton tip fit was confirmed on Jeanoko. No printed nozzle fit is verified. Current slots have a 100 mm hose spigot; hose/clamp selection is pending. The remaining bodies have no fitted connector.

CFD values below are temperature drops relative to each run’s ambient. Current slots use the best sampled 5 cm patch at 10 s. Older conical runs use a fixed centred patch in a steady field, with different source conditions and no gravity. These are separate comparisons. Early unconverged results are withheld.
