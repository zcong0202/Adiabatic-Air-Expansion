Product description
Cold air gun is very helpful for the application of quick dry cooling of small local areas, such as B. Cooling the machine tool head, cooling treatment of various cutting tools, electronic components cooling, cooling welding and so on, it is an ideal tool for cooling small areas. Functions: The cold air gun is the cold air source that is easy to transport. It is often used in mechanical drying or cooling. Only compressed air can be used to lower the air temperature and create an air flow at low temperature. Clean and easy to install, and its flexible universal hose delivers cold air to the place where it needs to be cooled. An important device for cooling applications in harsh industrial environments, which can work continuously and stably. It has a built-in control valve, so you can adjust the volume of exhaust air and temperature as needed. Specification: Material: aluminium alloy. Type: 08 no base, 08 with magnetic base (optional) Air pressure: 6.9 bar. Air consumption per minute: 0.2 m³/min. Cooling capacity: 450Btu/h. Length of cold air gun: approx. 13 cm Flexible tube length: approx. 21.2 cm. Weight: approx. 145 g ~ 220 g ~ 7.8 oz. Package list: 1 x cold air gun. 1 x flexible tube. 1 x magnetic base (optional).
