AA iterative cooling design computation — 15 October 2026

CONFIRMED OBJECTIVE
Mean air-temperature drop >6 deg C, relative to ambient, across a 50 x 50 mm
square 200 mm from the FINAL outlet. Still air first; wind next.
This is not a skin-temperature or heat-removal target.

WHAT IS VERIFIED
Read results/summary.json and each case/audit.json for current status.
Numerical acceptance and measured hardware validation are separate.
The old 3-D CFD screens are NOT used to fit this search.

NOMINAL SOURCE AND TEMPERATURE SENSITIVITY
Cold-branch flow 0.002 kg/s, source 5 deg C, ambient 35 deg C.
Mass flow is assumed. The reported 5 deg C nozzle reading does not establish
that temperature at this flow or with a new diffuser attached.
Temperature sensitivities use 5, 10 and 15 deg C at the same computed flow.
Actual paired source flow/temperature/backpressure data remain absent.
Do not apply the compressor's 6.9 bar directly at this CFD inlet.

COMPUTATION
1. Structured single-cell 5-degree rotational wedge, OpenFOAM v2506.
2. SIMPLEC incompressible RANS + passive thermal-deficit field theta.
3. T = Tamb - theta*(Tamb-Tcold), constant-density dry-air approximation.
4. Native boundary flux, scalar bounds, residual and full-field checks.
5. Gauss quadrature integrates the square target using radial CFD profiles.
6. PCHIP fits ONLY numerically accepted, comparable exit cases. No extrapolation.
7. CFD evaluations check interpolated threshold crossings. Failed cases never
   become training data merely because their reported cooling is high.
8. Internal cone flows test the ideal uniform-exit assumption.
9. Mesh/domain, turbulence and convection sensitivities are recorded separately.
10. Heat pickup and equilibrium moist-mixing are ONE-WAY sensitivities. These
    do not replace conjugate, variable-density or condensing CFD.

GEOMETRY SEARCH
Round outlet diameters 8,16,24,32 mm, with adaptive 22/23 mm threshold checks.
The fully turbulent exit-screen search excludes Reynolds number below 5000.
The 32 mm point and the 1 g/s sensitivity need transition-model caution.
For a 24 mm exit: 8 mm inlet, cone half angles 3,5,7 deg, 15 mm inlet land,
10 mm exit land. Proposed wall 2 mm. Proposed prototype length cap 180 mm.
These are assumed design bounds, not established installation constraints.
Distance remains 200 mm from the final outlet for EVERY shape.

TWO OPTIMIZATION QUESTIONS
search.py finds the smallest uniform outlet that exceeds 6 deg C with a
1 deg C margin at a 15 deg C source, within declared diameter bounds.
optimize_shapes.py compares accepted INTERNAL diffuser cases at source
5,10,15 deg C separately. It reports highest margin and shortest feasible
length. These objectives cannot establish a unique global hardware optimum.

NUMERICAL ACCEPTANCE
The exact fixed rules are in config.json. They include the last 30 relevant
initial residuals, source-normalized native mass/scalar flux, bounded theta,
last three target samples, and full velocity/scalar-field changes.
The wedge has no physical swirl. The normalized Uz residual can be dominated
by roundoff, so acceptance uses Ux/Uy and independently limits the absolute
Uz amplitude and changes of the ENTIRE vector field. Raw Uz residuals remain
in logs. A solver's printed convergence flag is recorded, not fabricated.
A supervisor requests clean stopAt writeNow only after all other gates pass.
Early numerical phases are retained in metadata/logs. Tighter pressure linear
solves improve the residual floor; the acceptance criteria are not relaxed.

THERMAL PENALTIES
Heat pickup: source deficit multiplied by exp(-U*A/(mass*cp)), with an
assumed overall U of 0,2,10,20 W/m2/K. Surface area follows the actual cone.
This is a lumped mixed-air estimate, not resolved wall heat transfer.
PsychroLib 2.5.0 supplies ASHRAE moist-air properties. Moist mixing conserves
humid-air mass and moist enthalpy; equilibrium condensate is retained.
Ambient RH40/60/80%, source RH10/100% are scenarios, not measurements.
Combined stresses use U20 and ambient RH80%, with saturated source air before
heating. The cold-stream humidity ratio remains fixed during heat pickup.
Uniform entrained-air stresses at 35,37,40,45 deg C test hot recirculation relative to the 35 deg C room reference. These are linear passive-field rescalings, not resolved hot exhaust plumes.
These stresses are illustrative bounds, not statistical confidence limits.

KNOWN MODEL LIMITS
Still-air axisymmetry excludes crosswind and gravity-driven jet deflection.
Constant density excludes the ~11% cold/ambient density difference at 5/35 C.
A passive thermal field neglects kinetic-energy conversion. A 33 m/s source
has about 0.55 K kinetic-energy temperature scale, smaller at the target.
Internal cone flow is included in shape cases. The external body is omitted
and the external lip is idealized as zero thickness; CAD has a 2 mm wall.
No source-response curve, external hot exhaust, person, clothing, radiation,
compressor energy, noise or subjective comfort model is included.
Wall-function near-wall sensitivity remains a limitation even with a finer grid.
Results are conditional design screens. No physical hardware optimum is proved.

RUN, FROM THE AIRFLOW-ASSESSMENT ROOT
Use the Python environment with requirements-cfd.txt.
  python optimization/scripts/verify_computation.py
  python optimization/scripts/round_jet.py my_D24 --diameter 24 --run
  python optimization/scripts/search.py --run --budget-new 2
  python optimization/scripts/optimize_shapes.py --run
  python optimization/scripts/report_search.py
  python optimization/scripts/thermal_sensitivity.py
  python optimization/scripts/update_search_overview.py

Use a NEW case name. Generators refuse to overwrite case directories.
Do not replay historical mid-run numerical changes as if they were fixed
settings. Generate clean cases, then assess the final solution with the gates.
For an existing stopped case, resume() resets stopAt to endTime explicitly.
The pinned Linux ARM64 solver image is recorded in every run-status.json.
Python and Docker scripts work locally, without paid remote CFD services.
