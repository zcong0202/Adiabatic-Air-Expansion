Motor + brake: printable static layout

This is not a powered, fully printed assembly. The model supplies an example base, two motor cradles, blank bearing supports and a blank brake adapter. Hardware envelopes are shown only to assess spacing. It has not been fitted to the actual motor or selected brake.

The AM0701 manufacturer publishes a 1–7 bar operating range. The scuba source is around 200 bar. Do not bypass pressure reduction. A partly open valve or orifice is not a pressure regulator; when flow stops, pressure across a restriction can equalize. Use a rated, flow-qualified pressure-control/protection package. Higher flow should come from an adequately sized supply path within the motor rating.

  • Base: example 300 × 180 × 8 mm. Slots position example parts. Printed structure only; metal fastening would still be required.
  • Motor cradles: example 80 mm body diameter, unmeasured. Not a verified AM0701 fit.
  • Brake supports: blanks with no bearing seats or actual caliper hole pattern. The brake model remains unresolved.
  • Coupler: spacing envelope only. No torque-transmitting STL supplied. Shaft diameter, usable length, key/flat/thread and retention are unknown; the earlier approximate 4 cm figure does not define a bore.

Printed structural and torque parts need strength, temperature, creep, fatigue, retention, alignment and speed validation. The earlier 400 W cooling calculation does not establish these ratings. A hot brake may soften PLA. No rotating containment or pressure part is supplied.

Four layout meshes pass closure/positive-volume checks. These checks do not establish fit or operating safety. Use the parts only as cold layout/measurement aids. Scope, source and next inputs.
