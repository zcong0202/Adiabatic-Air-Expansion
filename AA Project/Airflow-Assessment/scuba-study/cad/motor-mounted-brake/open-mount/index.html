Open brake support:189 ×152 ×18 mm. Motor plate:136 ×152 ×10 mm. Two flat-print pieces fit the H2S bed. No walls, cover or surrounding shell.

Motor holes and shaft/disc datums follow R07. IS brake adapter and caliper remain estimates. Five metal M5 reference bolts and8 mm sleeves connect the two printed pieces.
