Couple forces assume pure torque. Real bolts also carry net pad force, offset bending, preload and vibration. The 9 N·m case is not a certified safety factor. Stall and transient torque remain unknown.

Power and heat

P = T × 2π × RPM / 60   E = P × time

3 N·m at 2000 RPM is 628 W. At 3000 RPM it is 942 W, above the motor’s advertised 0.9 HP ≈671 W maximum. Do not combine separate published maxima as a rated operating point.

400 W for 10 seconds deposits 4 kJ into the brake system. Rotor temperature cannot be calculated reliably without rotor mass, heat capacity, heat split and cooling conditions. Spacers and vents do not prove thermal protection.

H2S print plan

The two STL files are translated onto z=0. Each is one closed connected solid. Largest footprint: 238 × 200 mm. The H2S build volume is 340 × 320 × 340 mm.

  • 0.4 mm nozzle; start at 0.20 mm layers, 8 wall loops and 8 top/bottom layers.
  • Use local solid infill around holes, webs and slot ends. Start with 40% general infill for the fit prototype.
  • Print the integrated cup on its flat motor face, opening upward. Print the lid flat underside down.
  • Inspect bridges over vents/cable opening and hole clearances in Bambu Studio. Use the filament maker’s profile. No sliced G-code is supplied.
  • PLA is for non-powered fit checks here. Prusa describes PLA as brittle and unsuitable for high mechanical or thermal demands. H2S-compatible engineering materials such as PC are candidates for testing, not a guarantee of safe operation.

What still controls release

Measure the Clarks adapter and align the rotor plane. Verify motor threads, bearing loads and shaft retention. Select a metal hub and a brake rated for the actual speed and duty. Qualify hot printed strength, creep and fastener preload. Provide a suitable metal backup load path and rotor guard before powered use.

The housing carries mechanical loads, not supply pressure. Keep the scuba supply regulated and protected within the motor’s confirmed rating. Do not bypass the regulator.
