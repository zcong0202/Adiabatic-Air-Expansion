Load path and assembly

Metal brake + stock adapter→Case lands→Case + five plate bolts→Motor adapter plate→Two motor holes

Mount the plate to the motor using verified motor threads. Attach the case to the plate. Install the stock brake adapter through the rear IS screw access. Fit the independently designed metal hub/rotor, align the brake, route the cable, then close the cover. This is a concept assembly sequence; access and actual hardware must be checked physically.

See build notes.

Metal rotor, retained metal hub, commercial brake, bolts, nuts, washers and compression sleeves remain required. The sleeve references are83 mm for the cover and8 mm for the motor plate. Exact fastener selection remains pending.

Open before powered use: real brake fit, motor port clearance, hub/shaft retention, braking duty and RPM, hot printed strength and a suitable guard. This case carries mechanical loads; it is not a pneumatic pressure vessel.
