Scale check

In one fitting-sheet panel, the40 mm bolt shank spans about118 pixels; the35 mm shank about101 pixels. A caliper long-axis span near333 pixels gives113–115 mm. Manual endpoints and perspective are uncertain. This supports an approximately110 mm envelope, not precision dimensions.

The160 mm item diameter in Amazon specifications refers to the rotor. It does not give caliper size.

Effect on the existing housing

The nominal estimate intersects the R05 housing and lid at the assumed pose. The previous small placeholder did not include these features. R05 housing STL downloads are withdrawn from the current pack pending a new fit design.

Conflicts involve the main body, adapter, rear adjuster, actuation arm and barrel adjuster. The case has38 mm internal depth; this estimate has59 mm axial extent before tool clearance.
