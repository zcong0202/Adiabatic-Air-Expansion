Design:
The design of the AM0701 air motor is long-lasting and easy to maintain. It also has long-life accessories, making it a reliable choice for various applications. In addition, the air motor is compact and portable, allowing for easy transport and storage.Specifications:
The AM0701 air motor has a high torque capacity of up to 26.6 in-lb / 3.0 N.m and a maximum rotation speed of 3000 RPM. It is suitable for use in harsh environments, with a maximum temperature of 120 C. These specifications make the air motor suitable for various industries and applications.Applications:The AM0701 air motor is suitable for the painting, printing, chemical, food, and baking industries. It can also be used for stirring ink and mixing various liquids. Its air-operated agitator design is cool running and non-electrical, making it easy to control and operate. The air motor also has high stability, making it a reliable choice for various applications.Mounting options:The AM0701 air motor offers a variety of mounting options, including round/square-cap mounting, screw-driven, adjustable drum cap, and portable manually elevatable options. These options allow flexibility in how the air motor is used and where it can be installed.
