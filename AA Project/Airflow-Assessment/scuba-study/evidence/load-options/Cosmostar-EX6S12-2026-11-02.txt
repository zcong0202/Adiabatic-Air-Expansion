🔄 Collapsible and Expanding Design: Crafted for convenience, the EX6 Mixing Blade easily inserts into the drum and expands upon rotation, ensuring efficient and thorough mixing of materials.

📏 Size Options for Comprehensive Mixing: Reduce manual stirring and save valuable time with these blades that offer comprehensive mixing throughout the drum. The size is 6" (EX6) when fully open.

🔧 Versatility and Compatibility: Designed to fit a 12mm (0.47") shaft, these blades are versatile and suitable for various drum mixers.

🌪️ Efficient Mixing: The blade's design and axial flow impeller ensure optimal agitation for all types of fluids.

🛠️ Durable Construction: Made of robust stainless steel, the EX6 blades offer longevity and resistance to corrosion. All wetted parts are also made of stainless steel for added durability and extended service life.

The Cosmostar EX6/EX10 Stainless Steel Collapsible Mixing Blade offers an ideal solution for effective mixing in 55-gallon drums, providing a seamless experience for handling paints, resins, epoxies, and more. Enjoy an upgrade in efficiency and performance with this versatile mixing blade.
