AA concept geometry and rough CFD study
15 October 2026

Purpose
Compare proposed delivery geometries at 200 mm from a cold jet.
Installed source: Jeanoko 08 micro + magnetic base, confirmed by user.
Milton S-183 and EXAIR small-tube assets are references, not replicas.

Four proposed concepts
D0_round: 8 mm bore, 40 mm straight passage.
D1_compact: 8 mm inlet, 6 mm exit; convergence over final 15 mm.
D2_slot: 8 mm inlet to 16 x 3 mm capsule slot, 40 mm total length.
D3_shield: D0 plus an open 26 mm ID / 30 mm OD shield, 30 mm long,
           starting 2 mm beyond the core exit. Mounting supports omitted.

Geometry units
concept.step and fluid-domain.step explicitly declare METRES.
wall.stl uses METRES and is intended for CFD.
wall-mm.stl uses MILLIMETRES for viewers/prototype workflows.
mesh.msh uses METRES, with named boundary patches.
Thread geometry is omitted. These are flow concepts, not fitted fabrication
drawings. A nominal pipe-thread size is not the flow-bore diameter.
STLs have been checked for watertightness and consistent face winding.
STEP scale has been independently checked by an OpenCASCADE import.

Assumed screening conditions
Cold mass flow: 0.002 kg/s.
Cold approximate total temperature: 15 deg C.
Ambient: 35 deg C. Crosswind: 0 or 1 m/s.
Source sensible capacity relative to ambient: 40.2 W.
These are assumptions, not measured Jeanoko performance or its seller rating.
Do not apply 6.9 bar at the cold delivery plane.

Reported target
An assumed 50 x 50 mm square, 200 mm from the primary jet exit.
The shield also has a sample 232 mm from the jet exit: this leaves 200 mm
clear of the shield mouth. Values are air fields and advected cooling
potential. They are not heat removed from skin or a thermal-comfort result.

Model
OpenFOAM v2506, Linux ARM64, finite volume RANS, k-epsilon.
The screening solver uses a Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy.
Constant density is used in continuity. Temperature transport approximates
total temperature and omits kinetic-energy recovery. Density variation over
20 K is about 6.7%; this is a model approximation, not validation.
Final screening convection uses first-order upwind. Numerical mixing is
mesh-dependent. Baseline and slot refined meshes are supplied separately.
The model excludes internal vortex physics, source/backpressure coupling,
condensation, radiation, a human body and compressor electricity.

Read the result status first
results/study-summary.json gives completion and convergence counts.
results/screening-results.json includes residuals, native boundary flux
checks, target metrics and exclusions. A small linear-solver residual does
not establish nonlinear convergence. Failed or nonconservative cases must
not be used to select a design. No validated winning geometry is claimed.
results/refinement-results.json records the finer-mesh checks.
Early ideal-gas, loose-tolerance and higher-order trials are diagnostics,
not accepted predictions. Workspace logs retain those attempts.

Files
designs/: CAD, surface meshes and named fluid volume meshes.
cases/*bouss*/: OpenFOAM dictionaries, initial fields and solver logs.
results/: actual sampled-field plots and JSON metrics.
evidence/: source records, geometry checks and numerical checks.
scripts/: generators, runner, post-processing and report generation.

Run environment
Python dependencies are pinned in requirements-cfd.txt.
Solver image and digest are recorded in evidence/computation-environment.json.
The scripts use Docker and a mounted work directory. No remote compute or
paid CFD service is required by this package.

Typical case commands, from this Airflow-Assessment folder
  python scripts/run_case.py D0_round_bouss_w0
  python scripts/postprocess.py
  python scripts/boundary_balance.py
  python scripts/report_results.py

The runner converts mesh.msh, checks the mesh, initializes flow and runs the
solver selected by case.json. Initial fields may contain solved warm starts.
The archive contains final case inputs and diagnostic outputs. Historical
mid-run numerical changes are recorded in metadata and logs; reproduce a
clean calculation with final settings, not the discarded startup attempts.
