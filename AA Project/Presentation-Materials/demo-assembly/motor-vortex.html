The branches are parallel. The vortex tube needs a compressed-air inlet and produces separate hot and cold streams, as described by EXAIR. Near-atmospheric motor exhaust cannot provide that inlet condition. This generic principle does not establish the Jeanoko unit’s pressure or flow rating.

Total delivered cold air is motor exhaust plus the vortex cold stream. Do not count only the vortex cold outlet when sizing tank consumption.
