Use the same Walfront VL53L1X board. Keep the ToF leads short. The included firmware uses a low-voltage software I²C bus; do not replace it with the stock Wire library. Valve connection using your BoM

The RobotGCC 5 V relay module switches the separate 12 V supply. Add the relay input to the existing D8 signal; keep the ToF, LED and D7/A0 loopback. No loose resistors are needed with the specified driver module.

ConnectionWire it this way
Relay logicUno 5V → relay VCC; Uno GND → relay GND; Uno D8 → relay IN. Match printed labels, not left/right position. Check any JD-VCC jumper against the module instructions.
Switched supplyUPS DC output set to 12 V → 1 A fuse near the UPS → inline isolation switch → relay COM. Relay NO → valve positive. NC unused.
Valve returnValve negative → UPS 12 V negative. Keep the coil current path off the breadboard.
1N4007 diodeAcross the valve coil: striped cathode → valve positive; plain anode → valve negative. Use the WAGO nodes, with the diode close to the valve.
Standalone logic powerUPS regulated 5 V USB output → Uno USB-B via a suitable data/power cable. PC USB replaces that connection during configuration.
The valve never connects directly to D8, D7, A0 or USB power. Before changing polarity, flashing or moving wires, turn OFF the 12 V valve branch. The diode direction is mandatory.

Confirm relay polarity first

  1. Keep 12 V valve power OFF. Power only Uno and relay logic.
  2. Inspect the module's H/L jumper or trigger label. Select the matching mode above. A fixed LOW-trigger module needs LOW-trigger firmware.
  3. Move a target into the ToF zone. Confirm the relay's NO contact closes when the app shows logical HIGH. Confirm it opens after the timer expires.
  4. Confirm the relay remains OFF during power-up/reset. Then connect the fused 12 V coil branch.

Valve and power notes

BoM valve: U.S. Solid USS-PSV00041, 12 V DC, normally closed, 1/4 in NPT, rated up to 0.8 MPa / 8 bar. Confirm the delivered label.

Check UPS barrel polarity with the listed multimeter. No maximum software ON limit applies; confirm the valve's continuous-duty rating. Start without pressure, then use correctly regulated air within the valve rating.

D7/A0 verify the Arduino logic wire. They do not measure valve position or airflow. The 10 m BoM cable is not an approved ToF I²C cable.
