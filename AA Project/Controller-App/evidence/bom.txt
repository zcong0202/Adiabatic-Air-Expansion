     Sensor wiring: HC-SR501 uses 5 V/GND/OUT; VL53L1X uses 5 V/GND/SDA/SCL. The four signals must remain
     electrically separate; never place different signals in one WAGO connector. Use short Dupont leads and the breadboard
     for the ToF desktop test. The 10 m cable is not an approved I²C run; reserve it for PIR until a separate long-distance ToF
     solution is validated.
     ToF connector check: The selected Walfront listing includes a separate male pin header. Solder that header to the
     board, then use female-to-male Dupont leads for a direct sensor-to-UNO connection or the listed jumper set and
     breadboard for bench testing. Adafruit 4209 is the correct STEMMA QT-to-four-male cable, but only for a sensor board
     with a JST-SH 1.0 mm STEMMA QT socket.
     Programming setup: Use the existing laptop, the free Arduino IDE and the listed data-capable USB-C-to-USB-B cable.
     No separate programmer, LED, resistor or extra capacitor is required for the first bench test; the UNO and relay module
     already provide status indication.
     Bench gate before installation: Solder and continuity-check the ToF header; read stable ToF distance values; verify PIR
     triggering; upload and verify the selected 10/20/30 s timing; test relay switching without the valve; then validate the fused
     12 V valve branch with its flyback diode. Install into the lamp post only after these checks pass.
     Owned component: U.S. Solid USS-PSV00041, 12 V DC, normally closed, 1/4 in NPT, 0–0.8 MPa, Cv 0.23; excluded
     from the total.
     Pricing: The Walfront VL53L1X page showed free delivery to Dubai Al Barsha on 2 October 2026 and only three units
     remaining at review. Stock and checkout dates can change. Dates marked * still require Dubai-address confirmation.
